Toolkit 11 · Peer Interpretability and Refinement Record

Teacher Guide

Reviewers record observable evidence before judgement. Creators then decide IMPROVE or KEEP.

Reviewer promptRecord
Without creator explanation, what do you think the map/data story is claiming?
Which visible element or value supports your interpretation?
Can you reconstruct the time rule/scale?
One Verified Strength
One Priority Review Finding linked to evidence
Creator decision: IMPROVE / KEEP + reason
Bounded refinement rule
If the creators choose IMPROVE, they make one evidence-linked change and check the same interpretation condition again. KEEP is valid when the evidence supports the existing design. Cosmetic preference alone is not a defect.		Bounded refinement rule
