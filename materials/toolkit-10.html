Toolkit 10 · Common Assessment Rubric

Teacher Guide

Use for formative assessment, project feedback or local grading. The rubric assesses learning evidence rather than the sophistication of the technology used.

DimensionDevelopingSecureAdvanced
Data literacyRecords/calculations contain gaps or require support.Data are structured; calculations and units are mostly accurate; limitations are identified.Data quality decisions are justified and variation/limitations are interpreted carefully.
Evidence-based reasoningClaims are mainly opinions or weakly linked to data.Claims cite relevant values and distinguish evidence from explanation.Claims compare evidence, qualify uncertainty and consider alternative interpretations.
Critical AI literacyAI output is accepted with limited checking.AI claims are checked and Accept/Adapt/Reject decisions are justified.AI limitations, assumptions and uncertainty are actively challenged using evidence.
Creativity & communicationVisual/product is partly understandable but design choices are weakly linked to meaning.Time-Map/data story communicates a clear evidence-based message with a usable rule/legend.Design choices reveal a meaningful relationship that a conventional representation may hide, while remaining accessible.
Equity & reflectionLittle attention to missing perspectives or scope.Names at least one missing variable/perspective and avoids overgeneralising.Explains how data choices shape visibility, access and interpretation, without inferring sensitive characteristics.
