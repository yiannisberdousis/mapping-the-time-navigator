Toolkit 9 · Differentiation for Ages 12–16 and Mixed-Readiness Classes

Teacher Guide

Universal access: allow oral, written or visual evidence; provide worked examples and vocabulary support; assign meaningful group roles; do not grade drawing skill or device fluency as proxies for conceptual understanding.

DimensionYounger / less experienced learnersOlder / more experienced learners
DatasetUse Route B/C; 4 core variables onlyLarger dataset; one or two extension variables
MathematicsCalculator; mean/median/range with worked exampleSpreadsheet formulas; compare mean/median; identify outliers/variation
GenAITeacher-mediated prompt or printed output; check one claimStudent prompt refinement; check multiple claims and alternative explanations
ReasoningSentence starters: “The data show…”, “I know because…”Distinguish observation, interpretation, hypothesis and causal claim
ProductSmall Time-Map with 3–4 pointsLarger map, multiple modes, comparison or scenario modelling
