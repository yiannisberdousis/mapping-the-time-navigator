Toolkit 6 · Embedded Equity Check

Teacher Guide

Use one question in every core stage. OPT A remains available for deeper investigation, but equity is not dependent on completing an optional module.

Boundary: students must not infer disability, gender, socioeconomic status, ethnicity, health or other sensitive characteristics that were not ethically and explicitly represented in the dataset.

Stage2-minute equity questionEvidence students record
CORE 1 / Part 1What might our dataset fail to capture?One missing variable, experience or access condition.
CORE 2 / Part 2Is the AI treating this sample as if it represented everyone?One overgeneralisation to accept, qualify or reject.
CORE 3 / Parts 3–5Does our story/map claim more than our evidence allows?One scope statement or remaining limitation.
