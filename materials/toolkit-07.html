Toolkit 7 · Time-Map Design Brief + Worked Example

Teacher Guide

The Time-Map must have a reproducible rule. A viewer should be able to understand why a point is placed where it is.

Worked rule: School = centre. Radial distance on the page represents travel time. Scale: 5 minutes = 1 cm. Geographic direction may be retained only as a secondary orientation cue; temporal distance controls proximity.
