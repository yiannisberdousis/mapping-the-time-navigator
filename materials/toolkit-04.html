Teacher Guide

Use before any GenAI-supported analysis. Students/teacher tick each statement. Five or six checks = READY. Fewer than five = REPAIR BEFORE AI.

CheckYes / Not yetRepair if needed

No identifying or sensitive information is present.Remove or aggregate it.
