Teacher Guide

This dataset can be used as Route C or as the teacher modelling example before students analyse their own data.

Worked human baseline: mean duration = 18.3 min; median = 16.5 min; range = 20 min. J02 is geographically farther than J03 but takes less than half the time. Therefore distance alone does not explain temporal proximity in this sample.
