C2 · 60 min

CORE 2: Human Analysis → Ask AI → Verify

GenAI role: Pattern-recognition and data-interpretation assistant; never calculator of record or final authority

Modular PathwayC2
C1C2C3OAOB
Teacher Guide

Overview

Overview

Students first analyse the class/group dataset without AI and write two evidence-based observations. Only then do they provide an anonymised table to the approved GenAI tool and ask it to identify patterns or possible relationships. Students compare the AI response with their own calculations and source data, verify each claim, and classify useful suggestions as Accept, Adapt or Reject.

Teacher Guide

Expected Student Output

By the end of this part, students should have produced or demonstrated:

  • Two human-generated observations with calculations or direct references to rows/values.
  • An AI claim-check table recording each relevant AI claim, supporting/contradicting evidence and status: Verified / Partly supported / Unsupported / Cannot determine.
  • An Accept / Adapt / Reject decision with a one-sentence justification and one identified limitation.
Teacher Guide

Key Teaching Moves / Implementation steps

1. Reconnect to CORE 1 and require two human observations before opening GenAI.

2. Model one claim-check: “AI says X; which rows or calculations support it?”

3. Use only anonymised/aggregated data.

4. Ask the bounded prompt below.

5. Students highlight every AI claim that refers to their data and verify it.

6. Students record Accept / Adapt / Reject.

7. Exit ticket: one thing AI noticed, one thing it missed or overstated, one thing the students still had to decide.

Teacher Guide

🎨 Creativity Opportunity

Students choose how to represent one verified pattern so that the representation adds meaning (e.g. ordering, symbol size, line weight, annotation). They must be able to explain what the visual choice encodes.

Teacher Guide

🔍 Critical Thinking Prompt

Which AI statement is actually supported by our dataset? Which is plausible but not demonstrated? What would we need to know before accepting it?

Teacher Guide

Suggested GenAI Prompt for Students

We have already analysed this anonymised journey dataset ourselves. Identify up to three patterns or relationships you notice. For each one: (1) cite the exact values/rows that led you to it, (2) state how confident the claim should be, and (3) name one alternative explanation or missing variable. Do not invent data and do not make claims about people that are not represented in the dataset.

Teacher Guide

📌 Teacher Note(s)

AI must not replace the baseline analysis. If the tool gives a calculation, students still check it using their own calculations or spreadsheet formula. “Cannot determine from these data” is a valid and often desirable conclusion.
