CORE 3: Build and Communicate a Data Story

GenAI role: Optional communication coach after evidence selection; no wholesale writing

Modular PathwayC3
C1C2C3OAOB
Teacher Guide

Overview

Overview

Students turn verified findings into a concise data story. They select the evidence that genuinely supports their claim, state at least one limitation, and communicate what AI contributed and what required human judgement. This core closes the short pathway with a product that can be understood by an audience who has not seen the raw dataset.

Teacher Guide

Expected Student Output

By the end of this part, students should have produced or demonstrated:

  • A 3–5 sentence evidence-based data story or short presentation.
  • At least one visual (simple graph, annotated table or mini time representation) that is accurate and clearly labelled.
  • A source trail naming the dataset evidence, the AI contribution (if used), one verification step and one limitation.
Teacher Guide

Key Teaching Moves / Implementation steps

1. Students choose one verified finding worth communicating.

2. Require a Claim–Evidence–Reasoning structure.

3. Students create one accurate visual and label units/source.

4. Peer check: can another group trace the claim back to evidence?

5. Students add one limitation and one sentence on the role of AI.

6. Present in 60–90 seconds or submit as a one-page data story.

Teacher Guide

🎨 Creativity Opportunity

Students decide the narrative order, visual emphasis and form of communication. Creativity is assessed by how effectively design choices reveal evidence, not by decorative complexity.

Teacher Guide

🔍 Critical Thinking Prompt

What is the strongest claim we can make from our evidence without exaggerating? What would make the claim misleading?

Teacher Guide

Suggested GenAI Prompt for Students

Here is our verified claim, evidence and limitation. Ask us three questions that would help us make the explanation clearer to a 12–16-year-old audience. Do not rewrite the final text for us and do not add new claims.

Teacher Guide

📌 Teacher Note(s)

Require traceability: every headline claim must point back to a calculation, row, graph or verified comparison. A polished presentation with weak evidence is not a successful outcome.
