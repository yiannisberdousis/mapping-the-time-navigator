OPT A: Whose Journey Is Missing?

GenAI role: Optional question-generator for missing variables/perspectives; no inference about individuals

Modular PathwayOA
C1C2C3OAOB
Teacher Guide

Overview

Overview

Students investigate whose mobility experience may be under-represented by the dataset or flattened by an AI summary. They test how conclusions change when attention is given to waiting, accessibility, cost, caring responsibilities, safety, transport availability or other contextually relevant factors. The aim is not to infer sensitive characteristics, but to recognise that “average journey” is not a neutral description of everyone.

Teacher Guide

Expected Student Output

By the end of this part, students should have produced or demonstrated:

  • A “Missing Perspective” note identifying one variable, experience or condition that could change interpretation.
  • A revised or qualified conclusion that avoids overgeneralising from the available sample.
  • One practical design/data decision that improves accessibility or representation without inventing data.
Teacher Guide

Key Teaching Moves / Implementation steps

1. Display the class conclusion and ask, “Who or what could disappear inside this statement?”

2. Students inspect the variables collected; they may not infer unrecorded personal characteristics.

3. Select one missing/underweight mobility factor.

4. If GenAI is used, ask it for questions, not demographic claims.

5. Students rewrite one conclusion, so its scope matches the evidence.

6. Whole-class share: different data can produce different but defensible stories.

Teacher Guide

🎨 Creativity Opportunity

Students redesign one variable, annotation or comparison so a less visible mobility experience can be represented without stereotyping or exposing personal information.

Teacher Guide

🔍 Critical Thinking Prompt

When we say “students usually…” or “the average journey…”, who might that sentence hide? Is the problem in the data, the sample, the AI interpretation, or our wording?

Teacher Guide

Suggested GenAI Prompt for Students

Look only at the variables in this anonymised dataset. Suggest up to four questions we should ask before claiming that these journeys represent everyone’s mobility experience. Do not infer gender, disability, income, ethnicity, health or any other personal characteristic that is not explicitly and ethically represented.

Teacher Guide

📌 Teacher Note(s)

Equity is treated as an evidence question, not a guessing exercise. Students should identify structural or contextual factors and limitations without assigning sensitive identities to classmates.
