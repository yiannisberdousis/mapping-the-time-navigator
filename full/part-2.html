PART 2: Interrogate Data with GenAI

GenAI: pattern-recognition / interpretation assistant

Full PathwayP2
P1P2P3P4P5
Teacher Guide

Purpose

Purpose: compare human analysis with AI-supported pattern recognition without surrendering judgement. Students enter Part 2 only with a READY dataset and two human observations.

Teacher Guide

Outputs

(1) human baseline;

(2) AI claim-check table;

(3) Accept/Adapt/Reject decisions;

(4) one limitation or missing-variable question.

Teacher Guide

Teacher moves

Teacher moves: freeze the dataset; model claim → evidence → status; use the same bounded prompt as Modular CORE 2; require exact evidence for every accepted claim; record disagreements rather than forcing consensus; close with QUESTION → CHECK → CHALLENGE → DECIDE.

Teacher Guide

Evidence gate

Evidence gate to Part 3: no AI-generated claim may become a project conclusion unless students can trace it to the dataset or clearly label it as a hypothesis requiring more evidence.
