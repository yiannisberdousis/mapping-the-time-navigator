FULL PATHWAY · PART 5: Peer Review, Refine and Communicate

GenAI role: Optional implementation/communication assistant after the human evidence-and-decision gate

Teacher Guide

Overview

Overview

Students close the Full Pathway by reviewing peer evidence, deciding what to Improve or Keep, making one bounded revision where justified, checking the revised product against the original evidence and communicating a final data story with an explicit limitation and equity reflection.

Teacher Guide

Expected Student Output

By the end of this part, students should have produced or demonstrated:

  • Peer evidence records with one Verified Strength and one Priority Review Finding.
  • A justified Improve / Keep decision and, if improving, one bounded revision.
  • A final Time-Map/data story plus verification note, remaining limitation and reflection on AI/human contribution.
Teacher Guide

Key Teaching Moves / Implementation steps

1. Pair groups and separate creators from reviewers.

2. Reviewers record observable evidence before judgement.

3. Creators receive one strength and one priority finding linked to evidence.

4. Creators classify the findings and choose Improve / Keep.

5. If improving, make one bounded change only.

6. Re-check the same evidence condition.

7. Final communication includes what the map shows, what it does not show, and how AI was verified. 8. Save/submit the final evidence package.

Teacher Guide

🎨 Creativity Opportunity

Students may refine one visual or communication feature only when the peer evidence shows that meaning is unclear or misleading.

Teacher Guide

🔍 Critical Thinking Prompt

What exactly did the reviewer observe? Is this a supported problem, a preference, an out-of-scope limitation or insufficient evidence?

Teacher Guide

Suggested GenAI Prompt for Students

We have decided to [Improve/Keep] this feature because [evidence]. If we are improving, give at most two implementation options that preserve our data, purpose and visual rule. Do not choose for us and do not add new claims.

Teacher Guide

📌 Teacher Note(s)

Protect the evidence chain. Reviewers may judge; creators may agree, narrow or reject the judgement, but nobody rewrites the recorded evidence. One bounded change is enough. Failed or partial verification is still valid evidence.
