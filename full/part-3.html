PART 3: Build Evidence-Based Conclusions

GenAI: optional counterargument/questioning partner

Full PathwayP3
P1P2P3P4P5
Teacher Guide

Purpose

Purpose: move from patterns to defensible conclusions. Students distinguish description, explanation and speculation; compare alternative interpretations; and decide which claims the dataset can support.

Teacher Guide

Outputs

(1) Claim–Evidence–Reasoning record for two candidate conclusions;

(2) one rejected or qualified claim with reason;

(3) one equity/representation check;

(4) selected finding for the Time-Map.

Teacher Guide

Teacher moves

Teacher moves: require students to state what the data show before why it may be happening; ask for a counter-explanation; test whether averages hide meaningful variation; explicitly check sample size and missing variables; if GenAI is used, ask it to challenge a conclusion, not generate the conclusion.

Teacher Guide

Evidence gate

Evidence gate to Part 4: selected conclusion is specific, traceable, appropriately cautious and includes at least one limitation.
