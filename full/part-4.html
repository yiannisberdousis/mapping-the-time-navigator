PART 4: Design and Build the Time-Map

GenAI: optional creative consultant after human design rule

Full PathwayP4
P1P2P3P4P5
Teacher Guide

Purpose

Purpose: transform verified evidence into a visual argument. Students define a temporal mapping rule, prototype it, test whether another group can interpret it, then build the final representation.

Teacher Guide

Outputs

(1) design rule and scale;

(2) prototype;

(3) Time-Map with legend, units and evidence annotations;

(4) short rationale explaining how visual choices communicate the finding.

Teacher Guide

Teacher moves

Teacher moves: require the rule before visual production; limit the first prototype to a small number of points/journeys; test interpretability with peers; distinguish aesthetic preference from evidence-related clarity; preserve accessibility through readable labels, non-colour-only encoding and alternative formats where needed.

Teacher Guide

Evidence gate

Evidence gate to Part 5: another group can explain the map rule and identify the intended evidence-based message without creator coaching.
